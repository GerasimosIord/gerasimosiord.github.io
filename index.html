About

Who I Am

I'm a biology undergraduate at the University of Patras, completing a thesis on gut microbiome dynamics in hematopoietic stem cell transplantation patients. I use Oxford Nanopore full-length 16S rRNA sequencing to trace how the human microbiome collapses — and sometimes rebuilds — after one of medicine's most extreme interventions.

Before my thesis, I spent two months as a bioinformatics intern at ΕΚΕΒΕ "Alexander Fleming", where I built R analysis pipelines across 797 clinical samples. I've also co-authored a book chapter on nutrigenomics and neurological disorders.

Outside the lab, I think a lot about the philosophy of scientific discovery, the nature of craft, and what it means to build something that actually matters — whether in research, software, or business.

Scientific Work

Active · BSc Thesis 2024–2025 Oxford Nanopore · Full-length 16S rRNA · R

Gut Microbiome Dynamics in HSCT Patients

Tracking the collapse and potential recovery of the human gut microbiome following hematopoietic stem cell transplantation — using full-length 16S rRNA sequencing to achieve species-level resolution across the transplant timeline. The findings reveal dramatic disruption: a near-total loss of diversity driven by antibiotic conditioning and immunosuppression, with opportunistic pathogens rapidly filling the ecological vacuum.
